Heed Privacy Policy

Effective November 10, 2026. Applies to the Heed Android app (dev.alx.heed) and the Heed Garmin watch app.

The short version: Heed has no servers, no accounts, no analytics, no ads and no trackers. Everything it knows stays on your phone and watch. The only time anything reaches us is when you choose to email us a problem report.

Who we are

Heed is made by Clumsy Clanker, an independent developer. For any privacy question or request, write to 0x2alx@proton.me.

What Heed uses on your phone

  • Bluetooth connection state ("Nearby devices" permission): Heed lists your paired devices so you can pick one, then watches whether that device is connected. It doesn't scan for other devices or track your location.
  • Your screen lock: to stop the alarm or disarm, Heed asks Android to confirm it's you. Android checks your PIN, pattern or fingerprint; Heed never sees them.
  • Notifications, full-screen alerts, display over other apps, exact alarms, unrestricted battery: these let the alarm ring and show its screen on time, even when the phone is locked or Heed is in the background.
  • Your alarm tone choice (Pro): Heed stores which tone you picked, not a copy of it.

What Heed stores, and where

In Heed's private storage on your phone: your settings, your linked device's name and Bluetooth address, your auto-arm rules, your trusted Garmin watch's name and ID, whether Heed is armed or ringing, whether you bought Pro, and a log of Heed's own events (armed, disarmed, alarm, watch messages, errors) with times. It's used only to run the app and to help you fix problems. Uninstalling Heed deletes all of it.

One exception: Android's backup to your Google account includes your Heed settings (linked device name and Bluetooth address, alarm options, auto-arm rules, and whether you've seen the intro tour) and the date you first installed Heed (which decides early access to Pro), so a new phone starts set up. Google stores that backup under its own privacy policy, and you can turn backup off in Android's settings. Heed's armed state, trusted watch, alarm tone, app themes and event log are never backed up.

You can also save a backup file yourself (menu, Backup and restore): it holds the same settings plus the app theme, and goes wherever you choose in the file picker. "Build with AI" copies those settings to your clipboard so you can paste them into an AI assistant of your choice; nothing leaves your phone unless you paste or send it yourself.

Purchases

Heed Pro is free for early users. Once paid Pro launches, it is a one-time purchase handled by Google Play. Google processes the payment under its own privacy policy; Heed only receives confirmation that Pro was bought. We never see your name, email or payment details. Heed's own code makes no network connections. Two libraries inside it do their own jobs: Google Play Billing talks to Google to process purchases, and Garmin's Connect IQ library talks to the Garmin Connect app on your phone.

The Garmin watch app

The watch app and the phone app talk through Garmin Connect on your phone, which Garmin operates under its own privacy policy. The messages carry only Heed's state and commands (armed, alarm ringing, stop, arm, disarm). While Heed is armed, the watch saves its own GPS position on the watch so it can point you back to where you left your phone. It keeps only the most recent position, never sends it to the phone or to us, and uninstalling the watch app deletes it.

Problem reports

If you tap Report a problem, Heed opens your email app with a message to us and a text file attached: the app version, your phone model and Android version, Heed's settings and status, your auto-arm rules, which permissions are on, and Heed's event log (which can include error and crash details). Device names in it are replaced by short codes such as watch#1, and Bluetooth addresses and watch IDs are masked. You see the email before it goes out, and nothing is sent unless you send it. We use reports only to fix the problem you reported, never share them, and delete them within 90 days after the problem is resolved. You can ask us to delete a report sooner at the address above.

Deleting your data

Heed by Clumsy Clanker keeps your data on your own devices, so you delete it there:

  • On your phone: uninstall Heed. That deletes everything it stored: settings, linked device, auto-arm rules, trusted watch, Pro status and the event log.
  • On your watch: uninstall the Heed watch app. That deletes its status and the last phone position it kept.
  • In your Google backup: the Heed settings and first-install date that Android backs up stay in your Google account until you delete the backup or turn backup off in Android's settings.
  • Problem reports you emailed us: email 0x2alx@proton.me from the address you sent the report from, with the subject "Delete my Heed data". We delete the report and the email conversation within 30 days and keep nothing. Without a request, reports are deleted within 90 days after the problem is resolved.

Your rights (GDPR and similar laws)

Heed collects no personal data on our side: there are no accounts or servers, and what Heed stores stays on your phone and watch, under your control (uninstalling deletes it). The only personal data we ever receive is a problem report you choose to email us. We handle it to answer you and fix the problem, which is our legitimate interest in supporting the app and yours in getting help; you decide whether to send it, and you can see everything in it before you do.

For any report you sent, you can ask us, free of charge, to see what we hold, correct it, delete it, or stop using it, by writing to the address above. We answer within 30 days. If you're in the EU, UK or a similar jurisdiction, you can also complain to your data protection authority.

Our email is hosted by Proton (Switzerland), so a report you send is stored there.

Sharing

We don't sell, rent or share any data. Apart from Google Play (purchases) and Garmin Connect (watch messages), no third party is involved.

Security

Heed's data lives in Android's app-private storage, which other apps can't read. Watch commands are accepted only from Garmin Connect and only from the one watch you trusted.

Children

Heed isn't directed at children under 13 and doesn't knowingly collect anything from them. It doesn't collect anything from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
